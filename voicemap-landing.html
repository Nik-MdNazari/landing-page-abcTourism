Ipoh, Perak

What there is to do in Ipoh

A tin-mining boomtown wedged between limestone hills, with the shophouse architecture, cave temples and coffee culture that came with the money. Most of it sits within a walk of the old railway station — the rest is a short drive out of town.

  • Heritage

    Old Town shophouses

    Concubine Lane, the Birch Memorial Clock Tower and two blocks of British-era facades, all inside a twenty-minute loop.

  • Outdoors

    Limestone hills and caves

    Cave temples cut into the karst on the edge of the city — Perak Tong and Sam Poh Tong are the two most walkers ask about.

  • Food

    Kopitiam breakfast

    White coffee, bean sprout chicken and caramel custard. Go early — the good places run out rather than close.

  • Street art

    Murals and back lanes

    The Mural Art's Lane and the Ernest Zacharevic pieces scattered across Old Town walls, most of them a few doors apart.

  • Markets

    Night markets and riverside

    The Kinta riverfront after dark, plus the pasar malam that sets up on a different street each night of the week.

  • Day trips

    Kellie's Castle

    An unfinished Scottish planter's mansion in Batu Gajah, half an hour south and the most photographed ruin in Perak.
